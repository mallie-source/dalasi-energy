About us

building reliable
industrial & energy
solutions

Dalati (Pty) Ltd is a 100% Black-owned engineering and industrial services company established in 2025. The company operates through specialized divisions focused on delivering innovative, reliable, and efficient solutions across Southern Africa. With leadership experience spanning mining, construction, logistics, and engineering, Dalati is committed to building sustainable, high-performance businesses that support industrial growth.

What we do

Find out which one of our services fit the needs of your project

Mining Logistics

At Dalati, we provide reliable mining haulage and industrial transport solutions built for demanding environments. From bulk material movement to on-site logistics coordination, Dalati delivers operational efficiency, safety, and dependable performance across every project.

Energy Solutions

We supply dependable power solutions including diesel generators, tower lights, installation, maintenance, and technical support. We help businesses maintain and interrupted operations through efficient and scalable energy infrastructure.

Industrial Transport

Our transport division specialises in the safe movement of heavy equipment, construction materials, and industrial loads. With experienced operators and modern fleet capabilities, we keep projects moving efficiently and on schedule.

Fleet & Equipment Support

We operate and manage high performance in industrial fleets designed for mining, construction, and infrastructure environments. Through preventative maintenance, real-time coordination, and skilled operators, we maximise up time and reliability.

Infrastucture Operations

Dalati supports large scale industrial and infrastructure projects with integrated logistics and operational solutions. We combine engineering insight, transport expertise, and energy support to deliver seamless project execution.

Industrial Engineering Services

We provide innovative industrial support services tailored to the needs of mining, energy, and construction sectors. Our focus is on operational excellence, sustainable growth, and delivering solutions that create long-term value.

FAQs

Answering your questions

Get in touch

What industries does Dalati serve?

Dalati provides industrial, logistics, and energy solutions across mining, construction, infrastructure, commercial, and industrial sectors throughout Southern Africa. We support projects that require reliable transport, operational efficiency, and dependable power solutions.

What logistics services do you provide?

Our logistics division specializes in mining haulage, bulk material transportation, heavy equipment relocation, fuel supply logistics, and on-site operational support. We are equipped to operate in demanding industrial and mining environments.

What energy solutions does Dalati offer?

Dalati supplies diesel generators, portable tower lights, installation services, maintenance, technical support, and rental solutions. Our goal is to help businesses maintain uninterrupted operations through reliable power infrastructure.

Do you provide support for remote or large-scale operations?

Yes. Dalati is built to support high-volume industrial operations, including remote mining and infrastructure projects. Our fleet capabilities, dispatch coordination, and operational support systems are designed for challenging environments.

How does Dalati ensure safety and compliance?

Safety is a core part of every operation. We follow strict operational and maintenance procedures, conduct regular equipment inspections, and prioritize compliance with industry safety standards to ensure reliable and responsible service delivery.

Can Dalati provide customized operational solutions?

Absolutely. We understand that every project has unique operational requirements. Our team works closely with clients to provide scalable logistics, energy, and industrial support solutions tailored to their needs.

How can I get started with Dalati?

You can contact our team directly to discuss your operational requirements, request a quotation, or learn more about our logistics and energy capabilities. We’ll help assess your needs and recommend the most effective solution for your project.
